# DinoSpace Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 1, 2026 · **App:** DinoSpace (`com.dinospace.kids`)

> **The short version:** DinoSpace does not collect, store, or share any personal
> information — from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no ads, and
> no analytics. The NovaSaur AI runs entirely on your device, and your conversations
> never leave your phone.

## Who we are

DinoSpace is made by Karthikeya Arikirevula, an independent developer. Questions about
this policy or the app: **dinospace.app@gmail.com**

## Information we collect

**None.** DinoSpace has no sign-up, no login, no advertising, and no analytics or
tracking software. The app does not ask for, collect, or transmit your name, email
address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal
information.

## The NovaSaur AI

NovaSaur is DinoSpace's built-in helper for dinosaur and space questions. It is powered
by Google's Gemma model running **locally on your device** (using LiteRT-LM):

- Questions you type and the answers you get are processed entirely on your device.
  They are never sent to us or to any server.
- Your chat history is stored only on your device. Delete it any time in
  *Settings → Clear Data → AI chat history*, or by uninstalling the app.
- NovaSaur is designed to only discuss dinosaurs, prehistoric creatures, and space, and
  never asks for personal information. AI answers are generated automatically and can
  sometimes be wrong — tap any answer and choose *Report this answer* to tell us.

## How the AI model gets onto your device

When you install DinoSpace from Google Play, the NovaSaur model (about 3 GB) is
delivered by Google Play as part of the app install, under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We receive nothing.

If the model is ever missing (for example on a version not installed through Google
Play), the app can download it directly from Hugging Face, a file-hosting service. Like
any web download, Hugging Face's servers process standard connection information (such
as your IP address) to deliver the file — see the
[Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy). No account is needed,
nothing about you is sent by the app, and we receive nothing.

## Information stored on your device

Saved (bookmarked) entries, quiz scores, your daily streak, which entries you have
viewed, and your NovaSaur chat history — all stored only on your device. Erase any of it
in *Settings → Clear Data*; uninstalling removes everything.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Internet & network state** — only to fetch the AI model if it isn't already on the
  device. The encyclopedia, quizzes, and AI chat all work fully offline.
- **Approximate location (optional)** — only if you allow it, and only to point Sky
  Tonight at your part of the sky (moon phase, visible constellations and planets). Your
  location is used for the calculation on your device and is never stored or sent
  anywhere. If you say no, Sky Tonight simply uses a general Northern-sky view instead.

## Children's privacy

DinoSpace is designed to be safe and enjoyable for all ages, including children. Because
the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal
information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). This approach
is consistent with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and PIPEDA
(Canada). The app shows no ads and contains no social features, chat with other people,
or in-app purchases.

## Third-party services

No advertising or analytics SDKs. The only third parties involved are Google Play (app
and AI-model delivery) and, only as the fallback described above, Hugging Face.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page and the effective date
will be updated, and significant changes will be noted in the app's "What's New" section.
