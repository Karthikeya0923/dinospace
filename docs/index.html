dinospace

Dinosaurs & space for curious kids — an illustrated encyclopedia that works entirely offline.

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Free · Android 8.0 and up · rated for everyone · no ads, no accounts, no data collected

a peek inside

what's inside

100 illustrated entries — 50 prehistoric creatures, 50 wonders of space, each with stats, fun facts and a story

Scan the sky — hold the phone up and it names the moon, the planets and the bright stars really above you

Dino battles — pick two creatures, or one you drew yourself, and see who wins on size, speed and bite

Quizzes — anywhere from five questions to a hundred, with a friendly explanation every time

Drawing studio — draw your own dinosaur or planet, give it stats, and send it into battle

Two themes — a soft pastel storybook by day, a hand-painted twilight for bedtime

finding your way

There is no sign-up and no setup. Open it and the bar along the bottom is the whole app.

meet Nova

Nova is an AI chatbot that lives inside the app. Ask how big a T. rex was or why Mars is red, and Nova does its thinking right on the phone — so it works on road trips, planes and campsites, and nothing you type ever leaves the device.
